# Contract Clarity

An AI-assisted contract review app. Upload a contract, see the risks, compare the versions.

## Why this exists

A single buried clause, unlimited liability, a one-sided termination right, an auto-renewal nobody diarised, can cost real money. Contract review is slow and easy to get wrong, and I wanted to understand firsthand what AI can and can't do about that. I advise and write on AI governance, and that work should be grounded in how these tools actually behave. Building this one taught me more than a year of vendor demos.

## Features

- **Document upload** — drop in a PDF or DOCX and it extracts the text and clauses
- **AI risk analysis** — flags potentially risky clauses and explains why they matter
- **Suggested language** — proposes safer alternative wording, tracked per clause
- **Version redlines** — side-by-side comparison between any two versions
- **Templates** — NDA, MSA, SLA and Employment Agreement starting points
- **Expiry alerts** — email reminders before contracts auto-renew

## Built with

React, TypeScript, Express, PostgreSQL, and the OpenAI API. Developed on Replit with AI assistance.

## Important disclaimer

Contract Clarity is a learning project, and its AI outputs are suggestions, not legal advice. AI models can be confidently wrong, which is part of what this project exists to explore. Any output should be reviewed by a qualified lawyer before being relied on.
